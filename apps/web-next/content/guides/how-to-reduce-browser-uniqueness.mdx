## Choose a concrete question

Decide whether you want to reduce a particular exposed field or diagnose a broken website. There is no population sample in this tool, so it cannot promise a reduction in your percentage uniqueness.

## Record the initial state

Run the relevant probe and save a baseline. Keep the probe version, browser and scope recorded. Copy or export a redacted summary if local storage is unavailable.

## Change one setting

Change one setting you understand, such as a browser privacy preference, then repeat the same test. A changed hash may reflect randomization, rendering differences or a changed environment; it is not automatically better.

## Check compatibility

Use the compatibility preset to inspect available WebGL and local storage behavior. If a required website stops working, consider the tradeoff for your task. Do not disable protections just to obtain a green result.
